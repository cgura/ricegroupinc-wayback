The Rice Group at COMPASS Earns 2022 Angi Super Service Award

for the 10th Consecutive Year

 

Award reflects company’s consistently high level of customer service

 

Plano, TX – January 25, 2023 –  The Rice Group at COMPASS is proud to announce that it has earned the home service industry’s coveted Angi Super Service Award (SSA). This award honors service professionals who have maintained high service ratings and reviews on Angi in 2022.

 

“These outstanding businesses have helped homeowners not only maintain their homes, but also evolve them into spaces that can handle life, work, school and entertainment under one roof,” said Bryan Ellis, senior executive at Angi. “Our homeowners’ consistent positive reviews make it clear: these are the top pros in our network. Congratulations to this year's Super Service Award winners.”

 

Angi Super Service Award 2022 winners have met eligibility requirements. Pros on Angi qualify for the award by obtaining 3 or more services-performed reviews in the previous year, maintaining a current and lifetime GPA of at least 4.5+ stars. The SSA winners must be in good standing with Angi and have undergone our verification/screening

 

“The foundation of our company is our wonderful clients.  We never take anyone for granted and believe each opportunity we receive is another chance to earn another 5-Star Review.  The Super Service Award from Angi is a highly regarded award that confirms that we remain focused on our clients’ best interests.  The highest award from Angi is a great distinction for our company, especially in the highly competitive North Texas real estate market.”

Brent S. Rice, Broker Associate #0395896, Compass RE Texas, LLC  #9006927
