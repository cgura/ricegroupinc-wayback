What The Rice Group at COMPASS can do for you as your buying team

Buying a house is a huge decision that can be intimidating so you'll want to have the best people possible by your side to help make the process as smooth as possible. Having the right team at your side that has your best interests at heart and can provide you with timely sound advice based on current local market conditions is crucial.

Our goal is to get your family into the best house at the best price point possible for your needs and lifestyle.

Our job when you buy a home is:

  • Answering any questions you have about the process, no matter what stage of the buying process you're in. We're your local market experts so please don't hesitate to ask us anything!
  • Showing you any properties in the area that meet your family's needs. This includes properties available through the multiple listing service (MLS), and also any unlisted properties that we find through our local contacts and other affiliations.
  • Guiding you through the entire home-buying process from helping you find the home of your dreams to connecting you with lenders to get the best financing available.
  • Advise and negotiate on your behalf to ensure that you get the best deal possible on the home you want. Having expert negotiators on your side that know the local market conditions is key to helping you to avoid costly mistakes when purchasing.
